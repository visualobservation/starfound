StarFound  寻域星空

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This policy explains how 寻域星空 (StarFound) handles your information. The app requires no account, uploads no data to any server, and keeps your information on your own device.

1. Information We Process

Location

Used to compute the azimuth and altitude of celestial objects for your location. Used locally only; never uploaded to a server.

Camera Feed

Used locally only, for star detection and alignment. Never uploaded or stored. A screenshot is written to your gallery only when you choose to save it.

Sensor Data

Gyroscope, accelerometer and magnetometer readings are used to estimate your phone's orientation, locally only.

Device Information

OS version and device model, used locally to adapt to different cameras and sensors.

Purchase Information

In-app purchases are processed and recorded by the app store billing system. We never access your bank card or payment account details, and we keep no payment records.

2. What We Do Not Collect or Share

  • The app requires no account; we do not collect your name, phone number or email
  • We do not sell or share your personal information
  • We do not upload your photos or camera feed
  • We do not collect data unrelated to functionality

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Location, sensor, image and settings data are stored only on your device
  • We operate no server that holds any of your data
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing app data in system settings, removes all local data

4. Policy Updates

Updates will be posted on this page; continued use constitutes acceptance.
